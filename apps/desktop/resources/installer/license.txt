You may install and use M. Player for personal, non-commercial listening.

PRIVACY
- M. Player works completely offline. It makes no network requests to the internet, has no telemetry, no accounts and no automatic updates.
- Your music files are only ever read. They are never changed, moved or deleted. Edited tags are stored in the app's own database, not in your files.
- The library database, artwork cache and settings are stored in %APPDATA%\Mohhey Player on your computer only.

SECURITY
- The installer asks for no administrator rights and installs for the current user only.
- The app runs a small local service on 127.0.0.1 protected by random per-launch secrets; it is not reachable from other computers.
